# Scam Shield: cover letter to the c0mpiled-17 judges

Seoul, 9 November 2026

Dear judges,

In 2025, Koreans lost a record ₩1.2578조 to voice phishing, up 47.2% in a year. Most of it (78.6%) went to scammers posing as prosecutors or the FSS. They win the same way every time: they cut the parent off. "This is a secret investigation. Don't tell your family." Then they take over her phone and walk her to the transfer.

Scam Shield puts the family back in the loop. Mom forwards any suspicious text, KakaoTalk message or call transcript. Our AI agent scores the risk. At 60 or above, it holds the transfer and alerts her adult child live. Only the child, from their own logged-in device, can release the money. The AI can hold money but never release it.

Banks are the buyer. A pending no-fault bill would cost them ₩1,098억–2,811억 a year (FSC estimate). It exempts a bank that "sufficiently warned" the customer. Every Scam Shield decision writes a timestamped warning record, which is that proof. Families use it free.

We built it today: Next.js 16, Supabase with row-level security and Realtime, and a Claude agent whose actions are set by fixed rules, not by the model. All 11 end-to-end tests pass. The bank hold is simulated and every demo message is synthetic.

We're looking for a bank partner for a sandbox pilot, and your hardest question.
